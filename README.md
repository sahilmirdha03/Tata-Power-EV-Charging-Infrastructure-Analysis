# Tata Power EV Charging Infrastructure Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing electric vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure performance using business, operational, customer, and financial data. The objective is to evaluate charging demand, station utilization, customer behavior, financial performance, and potential expansion opportunities.

The project was designed to support data-driven decision-making regarding infrastructure optimization, profitability, and future EV charging station expansion.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze charging sessions and energy consumption patterns.
- Evaluate station utilization and operational efficiency.
- Understand customer behavior and charging demand.
- Analyze financial performance and project profitability.
- Identify potential opportunities for infrastructure expansion.

## Dashboards

### 1. Business and Usage Overview
Analyzes overall charging activity and business performance.

**KPIs:**
- Total charging sessions
- Total energy consumed
- Total revenue
- Total active customers
- Average revenue per session
- Average kWh per session

### 2. Station Utilization and Operational Efficiency
Evaluates station performance, plug utilization, and infrastructure distribution across districts.

**KPIs:**
- Total sessions per station
- Sessions per plug
- Plug count vs. actual utilization
- Average kWh per station
- District-wise station distribution

### 3. Customer and Demand Behavior
Explores customer activity, income segments, EV models, and battery capacities.

**KPIs:**
- Unique customer count
- Sessions per customer
- Average kWh per customer
- Revenue per customer

### 4. Financial Performance and Expansion Strategy
Focuses on project investment, financial returns, payback periods, and expansion planning.

**KPIs:**
- Total CAPEX
- Annual revenue
- Annual EBITDA
- Total subsidy
- Average CAPEX per project
- Average payback period
- Adjusted payback period

## Key Insights
- Charging demand varies across hours of the day and days of the week.
- Customer segments show differences in charging activity and revenue contribution.
- Station utilization and infrastructure distribution vary across districts.
- Financial metrics such as CAPEX, EBITDA, subsidy, and payback period provide a basis for evaluating charging infrastructure investments.
